Which equation gives the mean photosynthetic rate, in μmol·m<sup>−2</sup>s<sup>−1</sup>, in terms of the photochemical energy available, in μmol·m<sup>−2</sup>s<sup>−1</sup>, during this period?

B) P(x) = 0.026x + 2
C) P(x) = 9.8x + 0.026
