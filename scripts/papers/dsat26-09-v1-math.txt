Q: The equation x + y = 1,440 represents the number of minutes of daylight (between sunrise and sunset), x, and the number of minutes of non-daylight, y, on a particular day in Grand Rapids, Michigan. If this day has 740 minutes of daylight, how many minutes of non-daylight does it have?
