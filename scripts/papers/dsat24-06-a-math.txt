Q: The equation d = 29w + 100 gives the total amount of money d, in dollars, that James plans to save w weeks after he starts to save. What is the total amount of money, in dollars, he plans to save in 10 weeks?
A) 340

P: 1/(cx) = x/128 + 1/c
Q: In the given equation, c is a constant. If the equation has exactly one solution, what is the value of c?
K: -32
E: Multiplying by 128cx gives 128 = cx² + 128x, or cx² + 128x − 128 = 0. One solution means the discriminant is 0: 128² + 4(128)c = 0, so c = −32.
