Q: In the given equation, k and t are positive constants. A solution to the equation is −22/7. What is the value of t?
K: 23
E: Factor out (x − k): 0 = (x − k)(7x + t − 1). The solutions are x = k (positive) and x = (1 − t)/7. So (1 − t)/7 = −22/7, giving t = 23.
