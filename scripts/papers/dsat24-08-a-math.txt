Q: Ari examined a set of 86 plants. The lightest plant had a mass of 2.8 kilograms (kg) and the heaviest plant had a mass of 6.2 kg. Chihiro examined the same set of 86 plants and also an additional plant with a mass of 11.7 kg. Which of the following must be true about the set of plants that Ari examined and the set of plants that Chihiro examined?
A) The standard deviation of the masses, in kg, of the plants that Ari examined is greater than the standard deviation of the masses, in kg, of the plants that Chihiro examined.
B) The range of the masses, in kg, of the plants that Ari examined is greater than the range of the masses, in kg, of the plants that Chihiro examined.

Q: In the given equation, <i>k</i> is a constant. The equation has exactly one real solution. What is the minimum possible value of 4k?
K: 227
E: Let t = 57 − x, which must be ≥ 0. Squaring gives t² − t + (57 − k) = 0. The smallest k giving a real solution makes the discriminant 0: 1 − 4(57 − k) = 0, so 4k = 227 (then t = 1/2, exactly one solution).
