If the length of <span style="text-decoration:overline">RT</span> is 63 units, the length of <span style="text-decoration:overline">LK</span> is 21 units, and the area of triangle <i>RST</i> is 819 square units, what is the length of <span style="text-decoration:overline">KT</span>, in units?
K: 52/3
E: Area (1/2)(63)(ST) = 819 gives ST = 26. Triangles SKL and STR are similar with ratio 21/63 = 1/3, so SK = 26/3 and KT = 26 − 26/3 = 52/3.

Q: The area of a rectangular region is increasing at a rate of 110 square feet per hour. Which of the following is closest to this rate in <u>square meters per minute</u>? (Use 1 meter = 3.28 feet.)
A) 0.56
B) 10.22
C) 0.17
D) 6.01
K: C
E: 110 ft^2/hr ÷ (3.28)^2 ≈ 10.22 m^2/hr, and dividing by 60 minutes gives ≈ 0.17 m^2/min.
#22
Q: If (<i>x</i> + 6)/(5) = (<i>x</i> + 6)/(11), the value of <i>x</i> + 6 is between which of the following pairs of values?
A) −7 and −5
B) 8 and 11
C) 4 and 7
D) −4 and 4
K: D
E: Since the denominators differ, the only way the two fractions are equal is if x + 6 = 0, which lies between −4 and 4.
