Q: A line intersects two parallel lines, forming four acute angles and four obtuse angles. The measure of one of the acute angles is (7x − 610)°. The sum of the measures of one of the acute angles and three of the obtuse angles is (−14x + w)°. What is the value of w?

A) (512p)/(3)
B) (224p)/(3) + 96
C) 56p + 192
D) 560p + 96
