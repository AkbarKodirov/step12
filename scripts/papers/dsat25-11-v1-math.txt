Q: If 9<i>x</i> + 4 = 67, what is the value of 90<i>x</i> + 40?
A) 7
B) 70
C) 130
D) 670
K: D
E: Subtracting 4 gives 9x = 63, so x = 7, and 90x + 40 = 630 + 40 = 670. (Or note 90x + 40 = 10(9x + 4) = 10 × 67.)
