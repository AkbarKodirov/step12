Our story

In Uzbek legend, Humo is the bird of happiness. Whoever its shadow falls on is blessed with good fortune.

Humo AI puts that bird beside every student preparing for the Digital SAT. Humo is a patient, clever tutor that explains, encourages and celebrates, and helps students from Tashkent and beyond reach the university they dream of.

The logo shows Humo with its wings raised, rising, and crowned with a four-point spark: the mark of intelligence that sets Humo AI apart.

Personality

WiseKnows the SAT inside out, explains it simply.
WarmEncouraging, never judging. Mistakes are part of learning.
AmbitiousAims high: top scores, top universities.
ClearShort sentences, real steps, no fluff.

Voice

"Nice! That's 3 in a row. Ready for a harder one?"

"Congratulations on successfully completing the assessment item."

The logo

The Humo

The bird of happiness with raised wings: four feathers on each side, five tail plumes, head turned to the right. Drawn in the Humo gradient, the same pink-to-crimson light as the 3D Humo.

The AI spark

A four-point star crowning the bird. It stands for intelligence and is the brand's main graphic element.

The wordmark

"Humo AI" set in Onest Bold, tracking −2.5%. On dark: "Humo" white, "AI" in rose. On light: "Humo" navy, "AI" crimson. The logo is never all red.

Colour in use

Proportion

Navy 58%Cream 24%Crimson 13%5%

Navy carries the brand; red brings Humo to life. Use crimson for Humo, "AI", key numbers and the one action that matters on a screen. Never make a whole layout red: the logo and pages are navy first, red second.

Humo the mascot

Humo is the student's friend: a light red little bird of happiness who explains, cheers and never gives up on you.

Construction

  • Body one pear shape, head and body together, light red (och qizil) lit from the top left.
  • Crest three crimson feathers; the AI spark floats beside them.
  • Eyes big, with navy irises and two white sparkles. Brows are short crimson strokes.
  • Tail three curled plumes in crimson, rose and petal: Humo's signature.
  • Medal a navy collar with the AI spark medal on the chest.
  • Wings soft, rounded; they do the acting. Cheeks are blush at 40%.

The 3D Humo on the website keeps its own look. The 2D Humo is for social posts, stickers, app icons and in-app illustrations, in the same crimson family.

Instagram

Rules

  • Logo top-left on posts, top-centre on stories, at least 64 px from the edge.
  • One headline, max 8 words, Onest Bold, white; a rose kicker line above.
  • Keep text inside the middle 1080×1420 of stories (the safe zone).
  • Humo bottom-right on posts, never covering text.
  • Carousels: same background on every slide, page number in rose.

Which file do I use?

  • SVG for the website, Figma, Canva and print shops. It's sharp at any size.
  • PNG for Instagram, Telegram, slides and anywhere SVG isn't accepted.
  • Transparent background: -on-dark files go on dark backgrounds, -on-light on light.
  • For printing, send the SVG together with this guide's colour page.
