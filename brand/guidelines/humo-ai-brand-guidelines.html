Our story

In Uzbek legend, Humo is the bird of happiness. Whoever its shadow falls on is blessed with good fortune.

Humo AI puts that bird beside every student preparing for the Digital SAT. Humo is a patient, clever tutor that explains, encourages and celebrates, and helps students from Tashkent and beyond reach the university they dream of.

The logo shows Humo with its wings raised, rising, and crowned with a four-point spark: the mark of intelligence that sets Humo AI apart.

Personality

WiseKnows the SAT inside out, explains it simply.
WarmEncouraging, never judging. Mistakes are part of learning.
AmbitiousAims high: top scores, top universities.
ClearShort sentences, real steps, no fluff.

Voice

"Nice! That's 3 in a row. Ready for a harder one?"

"Congratulations on successfully completing the assessment item."

Colour in use

Proportion

Navy 60%Cream 22%Gold 13%5%

Navy carries the brand. Gold is precious: use it for Humo, key numbers and the one action that matters on a screen. Crimson is a spice, never a main colour.

Humo the mascot

Humo is the student's friend: a round, golden little bird who explains, cheers and never gives up on you.

Construction

  • Body one egg shape, head and body together. Width : height = 1 : 1.2.
  • Crest three plumes, the AI spark floats above them.
  • Eyes large ovals (navy pupils, white sparkle) set on the upper third.
  • Belly cream oval with two soft feather lines.
  • Wings soft mitten shapes with three feather tips; they do the acting.
  • Cheeks crimson at 35% opacity, the only crimson on Humo.

The 3D Humo on the website is the same character in motion; the 2D Humo is for social posts, stickers and in-app illustrations.

Graphic elements

Use it as a bullet, a "new" marker, a rating star or to frame a key number. Always gold or white, never rotated.

Instagram

Rules

  • Logo top-left on posts, top-centre on stories, at least 64 px from the edge.
  • One headline, max 8 words, Onest Bold, white; a gold kicker line above.
  • Keep text inside the middle 1080×1420 of stories (the safe zone).
  • Humo bottom-right on posts, never covering text.
  • Carousels: same background on every slide, page number in gold.

Which file do I use?

  • SVG for the website, Figma, Canva and print shops. It's sharp at any size.
  • PNG for Instagram, Telegram, slides and anywhere SVG isn't accepted.
  • Transparent background: -on-dark files go on dark backgrounds, -on-light on light.
  • For printing, send the SVG together with this guide's colour page.
